# Universal evidence rules

- Tie each material factual proposition to identified evidence and include each relied-on document in `used_documents`.
- Prefer concrete facts over abstractions: names, dates, titles, portfolio area, transaction volume, savings, cash flow, percentages, participant counts, geographic scope, named projects, companies, reviewing bodies, voting rules, and selection criteria.
- If the evidence supplies a number, do not replace it with vague wording such as "significant results" or "substantial scale."
- When media coverage is relevant, identify the outlets individually and provide, when available: outlet name and short description, article title, publication date, URL, and the specific point the article supports.
- Treat original and English translation as one evidentiary bundle. The original is listed first and the English translation second.
- A visual or unreadable original may still be cited by its indexed English title when its linked readable translation supplies the informational content. Do not discuss the technical limitation in petition text.
- Evidence gaps belong only in the structured internal arrays, never in `draft_text`.
- Do not evaluate the record from the adjudicator's perspective. Use the record affirmatively to demonstrate the applicable requirement.
