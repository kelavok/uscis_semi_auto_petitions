# Migrator EB-1A RFE response task rules

This task is a targeted RFE response using the Migrator company template, not a new EB-1A petition.

Priority order:

1. Runtime/user instructions in the prompt.
2. `user_case_instructions.md`, the accepted strategy manifest, and `rfe_response_plan.md`.
3. Exact RFE text mapped to the current drafting unit.
4. Initial-filing memorandum text for the same issue.
5. New indexed RFE evidence assigned to the unit.
6. Migrator RFE template and universal drafting rules.

For every response unit:

- answer only the disputed element and begin with the officer's actual concern;
- preserve the exact RFE quotation supplied by the strategy manifest;
- identify what was already argued or cited in the initial filing without presenting it as new evidence;
- identify the new evidence and explain precisely how it cures the stated defect;
- use clear factual subheadings at the episode/project level;
- close each criterion with a concrete conclusion tied to the rebutted elements;
- preserve document IDs and exhibit references, and flag unsupported assertions.
- set `include_rfe_quote` to `true` only when the accepted human strategy calls for an RFE quotation in that section; criterion rebuttals may use it, but final merits and the conclusion default to `false`;

Structural constraints:

- The INDEX is generated by the script and remains at the front of the document.
- Top-level exhibits are numbered consecutively from Exhibit 1 in first-appearance order; the statutory criterion number remains in the exhibit title and never controls the exhibit number.
- Do not draft a standalone Recommendation Letters section.
- Do not draft Industry Overview unless the accepted manifest maps an RFE issue and strategy to that section.
- Do not draft U.S. employment, continued-work, or prospective-benefit material unless the accepted manifest maps an RFE issue and strategy to that section.
- Do not repeat favorable or undisputed findings merely to lengthen the response.

If the human strategy conflicts with a generic template example, follow the human strategy and record the conflict in `revision_notes`.
