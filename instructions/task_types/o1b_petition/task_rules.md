# O-1B petition task rules

- Follow the company memorandum order and the configured `o1b_track`.
- For Arts use the distinction standard and O-1B Arts terminology. For MPTV use extraordinary-achievement terminology.
- Never import EB-1A, I-140, permanent-residence, NIW, or final-merits language into the O-1B draft.
- Claim only configured criteria. Comparable evidence is prohibited for MPTV.
- Draft one evidence episode per prompt. Criteria (iii) and (vi) use two prompts for the same episode id.
- Distinguish productions/events under Criterion (i) from organizations/establishments under Criterion (iii).
- Preserve the company section order. Use exhibit and page placeholders until deterministic pagination.
- Do not invent a consultation, contract, itinerary event, salary, publication status, award, audience figure, ranking, or future engagement.
