# Petitioner or agent support letter

Draft a standalone letter on behalf of the petitioner or agent, not a memorandum subsection. Identify the signatory and petitioner relationship; summarize concrete achievements relevant to the proposed work; explain why the offered role requires the beneficiary's demonstrated extraordinary ability or achievement; and state the position, project or engagements, dates, locations, compensation, duties, supervision/control facts, and supporting exhibits. Do not promise facts absent from contracts or case instructions.
