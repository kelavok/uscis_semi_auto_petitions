# Comparable evidence episode

Draft only the selected episode from the `comparable evidence` folder.

This section covers documented circumstances, achievements, professional recognition, impact, or other favorable facts that strengthen the overall picture of the beneficiary but do not formally meet an EB-1A regulatory criterion. Explain concretely what happened, the beneficiary's role, why the evidence is favorable, and how it supports the petition's totality and final-merits analysis.

Do not relabel the evidence as satisfying a regulatory criterion. Do not claim an eleventh criterion. Do not use the statutory “comparable evidence” doctrine unless the case-specific instructions expressly direct that legal argument. Preserve one drafting unit per folder-defined episode and cite every factual proposition to the indexed documents selected for this episode.
