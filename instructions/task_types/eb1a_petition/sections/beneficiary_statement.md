# Migrator EB1A beneficiary statement

Draft a separate first-person statement by the beneficiary. The output should
read like a document the beneficiary can sign, not like a legal memorandum
section.

Required structure:

1. Start with a respectful USCIS salutation or a short self-introduction.
2. State the beneficiary's name, field, specialization, and intention to
   continue working in the claimed area of expertise in the United States.
3. Describe concrete U.S. plans, projects, collaborations, employment,
   business, research, educational, or industry-development activity supported
   by the record.
4. Explain why those plans are beneficial to the United States in practical
   terms.
5. Refer to attached business-plan, employment-plan, recommendation, or
   supporting documents only when they are included in the Technical document
   selection.
6. End with a concise respectful closing and the beneficiary's typed name.

Use first person (`I`, `my`). Do not write internal attorney notes, technical
document IDs, unsupported promises, or placeholders that should be filled by a
lawyer unless the evidence genuinely lacks the information.
