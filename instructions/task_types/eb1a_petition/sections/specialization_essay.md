# Specialization essay

Draft the specialization essay according to the EB1A template.

The prompt may ask the LLM to search for supporting sources, prioritizing:

1. U.S. government sources;
2. major U.S. media;
3. research papers and institutional reports;
4. other credible sources.

Typical content:

- definition of the specialization;
- why it matters economically or professionally;
- practical effects and beneficiaries;
- value for adjacent fields or sectors where relevant.

All sourced propositions must include source links or be flagged for review.
