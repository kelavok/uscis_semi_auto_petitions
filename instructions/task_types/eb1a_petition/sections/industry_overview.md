# Migrator EB1A industry overview

Draft this section as a human-facing memorandum section titled or suitable for
`OVERVIEW OF THE INDUSTRY`.

Required content:

1. Identify the beneficiary's field and narrower specialization in plain
   English.
2. Explain why this specialization matters to the United States. Prefer U.S.
   government sources, official reports, major industry reports, and credible
   institutional materials when the prompt includes or requests external
   research.
3. Connect the industry discussion back to the beneficiary's record. Use the
   supplied documents and recommendation evidence only for supported facts.
4. Keep the section affirmative and attorney-style: it should help frame the
   later final merits analysis, not merely define the industry.
5. If the evidence contains future U.S. plans, mention them only briefly here;
   the detailed work-plan discussion belongs in the final merit / beneficiary
   statement units.

Do not include internal drafting notes, source-selection commentary, raw URLs
without context, or technical document IDs in `draft_text`.
