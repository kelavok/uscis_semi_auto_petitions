# Criterion i: awards episode

Draft final petition text affirmatively demonstrating receipt of a nationally or internationally recognized prize or award for excellence.

Required construction:

1. Identify the award, date, category/nomination, recipient, and awarding organization.
2. Provide the Exhibit 1 reference and complete numbered list using the item prefix supplied in the prompt.
3. Explain the award's purpose, eligibility, selection standards, reviewing body, voting or evaluation procedure, scope, and frequency using concrete evidence.
4. Explain why the beneficiary received the award and connect the recognized work to the beneficiary's specialization.
5. Use measurable facts from award letters and supporting evidence, including participant counts, geographic scope, portfolio size, savings, transaction value, or other documented outcomes.
6. If media evidence is supplied, add a `Media Coverage` subsection. List each relevant outlet separately with outlet description, article title, date, URL, and what the article shows about the award or its recognition.
7. End with a direct affirmative Criterion i conclusion.

Never use internal workflow language or discuss evidence-processing limitations in petition text.
