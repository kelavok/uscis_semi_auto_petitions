# Criterion ii: membership in associations

Draft final petition text affirmatively demonstrating membership in an association in the field that requires outstanding achievements of its members, as judged by recognized experts.

Use this order:

1. concise affirmative introduction addressing all Criterion ii legal elements;
2. Exhibit 2 reference and complete numbered membership-document list using the item prefix supplied in the prompt;
3. membership evidence;
4. relationship between the association/field-specific committee and the beneficiary's field;
5. concrete membership requirements showing achievement-based admission;
6. admission and review process, including who reviewed, what was considered, and who approved;
7. beneficiary-specific basis for admission, emphasizing measurable achievements from official letters or protocols;
8. association background and professional significance;
9. direct affirmative Criterion ii conclusion.

Use one English association name and one abbreviation throughout. Define a Russian acronym once only if needed to identify a document. Do not switch among inconsistent forms.

Use concrete membership requirements and applicant facts whenever documented, including years of experience, major projects, recommendations, proprietary developments, conference participation, approval bodies, portfolio size, transaction volume, savings, cash flow, named companies, geographic scope, and leadership titles.

If a gap exists, make the strongest accurate affirmative argument supported by the supplied record and put the gap only in the structured internal arrays. Never insert negative sufficiency analysis into petition text.
