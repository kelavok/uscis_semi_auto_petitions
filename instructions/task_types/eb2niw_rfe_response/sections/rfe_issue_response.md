# EB-2 NIW RFE issue response

Draft exactly one strategy-manifest unit. Its assigned RFE quotations and strategy control its scope.

Use this order unless the accepted strategy expressly requires another:

1. Filing-ready heading.
2. Exact RFE concern or a faithful lead-in to the quote already placed by the script.
3. `Answer:` with the direct proposition that resolves the defect.
4. Relevant continuity with the initial filing.
5. New documentary evidence, in logical order.
6. Explanation connecting each material fact to the disputed EB-2 NIW element.
7. A unit-specific conclusion.

Keep these boundaries:

- Basic Eligibility: Advanced Degree or Exceptional Ability under the applicable route.
- Prong 1: the specific proposed endeavor, substantial merit, and prospective national importance.
- Prong 2: whether this beneficiary is well positioned to advance that endeavor.
- Prong 3: whether, on balance, waiving the job offer and labor certification requirements benefits the United States.

Use the full RFE and initial filing for consistency, but answer only the assigned issues. Do not cite the RFE, strategy, initial memorandum, README, INFO, or extracts files as evidence. Use all and only the supplied `DOC####` evidence IDs in `used_documents`.
