# Prong 3 — on-balance waiver analysis

Apply the Dhanasar balancing considerations to the specific record. Address why the nature and implementation of the endeavor, the beneficiary's positioning and progress, relevant urgency or impracticality of ordinary labor-certification sponsorship, and prospective U.S. benefit favor a waiver. Do not argue that no U.S. worker can perform the work, that the beneficiary is irreplaceable, or that labor certification is categorically impossible.
